Support

Write to dmitrii.raev@gmail.com. This is one person rather than a support desk, so a reply takes a few days — but it comes. Include your device model, your OS version and the game version (Settings, at the bottom of the screen).

My progress is gone

Progress is stored only on your device and there is no server copy, so deleting the game deletes it for good. If the game is still installed, write in — sometimes the profile is intact and it is the backup that is being read.

A purchase was not credited

Restart the game with the network on: an unfinished order is redelivered by the store on the next launch and the gems are credited then. If they are still missing, write in and include the receipt from Apple or Google.

Chest odds

Chests are bought with in-game coins only, never with money directly. A chest draws one species from a pool, and every species in that pool is equally likely. The size of the pool, and how many of its species you do not have yet, are printed on the chest in the shop, before you buy.

Turn off usage data

Settings, then "Anonymous usage data". It takes effect immediately, not at the next launch.

Change advertising consent

Settings, then "Ad privacy settings". That row appears where the consent form was shown — the EEA, the UK and Switzerland.

Erase everything

Settings, then "Reset progress". There is no undo.
