Deepdrop

A puzzle about going down. Place the blocks, clear the lines, get deeper than you did last time. Lum's lantern reaches exactly as far as you are careful.

Support

Questions, bugs, ideas — write to dmitrii.raev@gmail.com. If the game crashed, include your device model and OS version; that is usually enough. Common questions are answered on the support page.

Data

No sign-up, and no servers of ours — progress lives on your device. The game sends anonymous statistics and crash reports through Firebase, and one setting switches both off. Ads are rewarded only, and always optional. Full privacy policy.
